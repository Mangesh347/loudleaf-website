Privacy Policy

Last updated October 1, 2026

The short version: Loudleaf reads a page only when you ask it to, the text is spoken by your own browser, and we never receive what you listen to. We only know who you are if you sign in with Google to buy Pro.

Who we are

Loudleaf is a Chrome extension and website made by Fenwick Labs ("we"). Privacy questions: support@fenwicklabs.app.

Page text

When you press play (toolbar button, keyboard shortcut, or right-click menu), Chrome gives Loudleaf temporary access to that one tab. Loudleaf finds the article text on the page and passes it to Chrome's text-to-speech engine. The text is not stored and is never sent to Fenwick Labs.

Voices are provided by Chrome and your operating system. Some "natural" voices are online voices run by Google or Microsoft; when you choose one, the sentence being spoken is processed by that provider under its own privacy policy, the same as when any website uses that voice. Choose an offline voice if you prefer nothing to leave your computer.

What stays on your computer

  • Your settings: voice, speed, highlighting, reader view preferences, and pronunciation dictionary.
  • Listening stats: words, minutes, and pages per day. No page addresses or text are kept.

Who we share data with

Only the processors that run the service: Supabase, Vercel, PayPal, Razorpay, and Google (sign-in). No ads, no tracking pixels, no data sales.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

Loudleaf's use of information follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data and your rights

Removing the extension deletes everything stored on your computer. To delete your account and purchase records (except what tax law requires), email us from the address you signed in with. Depending on where you live (GDPR, CCPA), you can also ask to see, correct, or export your data.

Children

Loudleaf isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
