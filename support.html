Start listening

  • Click the Loudleaf button and press the big play button, or press Alt+Shift+R on any page.
  • To read just part of a page, select it and press Alt+Shift+S, or right-click → Read selection aloud.
  • If you've scrolled down, Loudleaf starts from what's on your screen.
  • Alt+click any sentence to jump there.

There's no sound

  • Check that the tab isn't muted and your volume is up.
  • Pick another voice in the player (the speed button opens voice choices). Online "natural" voices need an internet connection.
  • On Windows, add voices in Settings → Time & language → Speech. On Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content.

Words aren't highlighted one by one

A few voices only report where each sentence starts. Loudleaf then highlights the sentence. Voices marked "word highlight" in Options → Voices light up every word.

PDFs, Google Docs, and Chrome pages

Chrome's PDF viewer, Google Docs, the Chrome Web Store, and chrome:// pages don't allow extensions to read them. Copy the text into a normal page, or select text elsewhere and press Alt+Shift+S.

Pro isn't showing after I paid

  1. Open Loudleaf → Options → Account and click I already paid.
  2. Sign in with the same Google account you paid with.
  3. Still stuck? Email us your payment ID and we'll fix it the same day.
