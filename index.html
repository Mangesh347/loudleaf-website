Six things Loudleaf does

Press any track to hear it.

  1. Every word, lit up

    Your eyes and ears stay together. The sentence you're on glows softly, and the word being spoken lights up in amber, so you never lose your place, even when you look away for a moment.

  2. Faster than you read

    Most people read about two hundred and fifty words a minute. With a little practice, you can listen at twice that. Loudleaf goes up to three and a half times normal speed, and your ears adjust faster than you'd expect.

    0.5× – 3.5×Changes instantlyRemembers your speed

  3. Only the article

    Loudleaf finds the story and skips the menus, the ads, and the comment section. With Pro, it also skips citation marks and reads web addresses as simply "link".

  4. A calmer page

    Reader view turns any cluttered page into clean, large text in a typeface designed for readers with low vision. Choose paper, night, or high contrast, and add a reading ruler that follows your pointer.

    Atkinson HyperlegiblePaper · Night · High contrastReading ruler

  5. Say it right

    Teach Loudleaf the names and jargon from your world. Tell it once that S Q L is "sequel", and it will say it that way everywhere, forever.

  6. Private, and yours

    Loudleaf uses the voices built into Chrome and your computer. What you listen to is never sent to us, there's no account to create, and it only touches a page when you press play.

Questions

Which voices does it use?

The voices that come with Chrome and your computer, including Google's and Microsoft's natural online voices when available. They cost nothing extra and new ones arrive with system updates.

Does it work on PDFs and Google Docs?

Not yet. Chrome's PDF viewer and Google Docs draw their own text, so extensions can't read them. Select text on any normal page and press Alt+Shift+S to read just that part.

Is my reading private?

Yes. Loudleaf runs only on the page where you press play, and the text is spoken by your browser. We never receive it. Listening stats stay on your computer.

Do I need an account?

No. You only sign in with Google if you buy Pro, so your plan follows you to other computers.

What happens when Pro ends?

Nothing renews automatically. Loudleaf goes back to Free: unlimited listening up to 1.5×.
